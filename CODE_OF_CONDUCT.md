# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe, and harassment-free community
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of
expertise.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and deliberately
disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[mujakayadan@gmail.com](mailto:mujakayadan@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed
promptly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly
representing the YARBA project.

This policy is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
